## Why each objection video is built the way it is
- **O1 - THE CHEAPER PLATFORM (price)**: Luke Iha "relevance in the first 10-20 words" - the hook now opens on their own sentence instead of Carson's intro; CTA swapped to a specific artifact (quote / invoice).
- **O2 - WHAT IF YOU DISAPPEAR? (security + ownership)**: Schwartz "interest + believability" - the answer leans on a physical fact (the named-owner clause on screen) instead of a promise; CTA is one keyword reply.
- **O3 - ARE YOU BIG ENOUGH? (young company)**: marketing-psychology social proof + Pratfall (admit the worry is fair) - proof is something they check themselves, and the ask is a one-word reply.
- **O4 - CASE STUDY: WHY THEY SWITCHED**: tessic-paid-acquisition "specific stories over generic claims" - each take opens on the client's own line, and the ask names the exact detail Carson needs to match them.
- **O5 - SIDE-BY-SIDE SWITCH**: marketing-psychology status-quo bias - names the specific fear of switching (patients can't reorder) and shrinks it into steps; ask is two concrete facts.
- **O7 - WHO BRINGS THE PATIENTS?**: tessic-paid-acquisition "disqualifies openly" - leads with the hard no, which earns trust for the rest; ask requests one concrete number.
- **O8 - ANOTHER BUSINESS TO RUN? (time)**: Luke Iha "constraints handled (time)" - the time constraint is answered with an explicit ours/yours split, without calling it easy (compliance).
- **O9 - THE MONEY RIGHT NOW (budget within 30 days)**: marketing-psychology mental accounting - the big number is reframed as $4,000 down plus a schedule, with the 4-week guarantee and its condition stated out loud.
- **O10 - BRING YOUR PARTNER (decision maker)**: copywriting "specificity over vagueness" - "things get lost" is made concrete (prices rounded, ownership fuzzy); ask is a specific new time.
- **O11 - JUST LOOKING (timeline 1-3 months / exploring)**: Schwartz gradualization - asks for a small yes (one question) and uses the real 4-week build time as the only urgency, no invented scarcity.
